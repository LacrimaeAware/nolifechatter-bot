# Provenance

This repository is the public edition of NoLifeChatter, exported from the
author's private working repository on 2026-09-17 with fresh history.

Included: the bot runtime (`chatbot.py`, `config.py`, `handlers.py`, the
command dispatcher and the `commands/`, `services/` and `utils/` packages),
the offline tools under `tools/` (setup, archive, artifact builds, checks,
maintenance), the Windows launchers, the LoRA path and evaluation harnesses
under `experiments/`, the synthetic examples, the reference docs and Pages
site under `docs/`, the unit tests, sample configuration and the license.

Left out: the author's research notes and operator ledgers, dated audits and
handoffs, research probes and labeling tools, the text-IQ experiment and its
`~iq` command, RunPod smoke-test tooling, and downloaders for third-party
chat-log mirrors. Runtime data (archives, settings, tokens, artifacts) never
left the private machine; `data/` here holds only placeholders.

Developed with AI assistance; design decisions, reviews and the claims in
these documents are the author's.
