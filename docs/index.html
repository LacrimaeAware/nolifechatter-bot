NoLifeChatter

A Twitch bot for translation, language practice and replies in a chosen chatter's style.

Persona replies use retrieved messages from the chosen chatter and the current conversation.

Example walkthroughBot reference

Model personas

The bot checks for copied lines and ranks candidates for relevance and style.

Generation and channel modes · Optional LoRA training

~persona chatter_01 sourdough
Generate one reply using this chatter's history.
~generate chatter_01 chatter_02 optimist topic=speedruns
Combine writing examples from two chatters with a trait and topic.
~botpersona chatter_01 mode=response
Keep a persona active and reply when the bot is mentioned.

Embeddings and chat analysis

Message embeddings represent passages numerically. The bot can retrieve related passages by vector similarity as well as keyword matches.

~askchat user=chatter_01 sourdough
Answer questions from this chatter's saved messages. Raw mode returns the matching messages.

    Translation

    Translate chat through DeepL or Google Cloud. Practice mode whispers translations of your native-language messages into study languages. It translates your study-language messages into your native language in chat. Japanese, Korean and Thai can also be romanized.

    ~practice on es,ja en

    Practise Spanish and Japanese with English as your native language.
